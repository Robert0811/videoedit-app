VideoEdit

A personal desktop tool for editing videos and uploading them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Edits video files on the owner's computer (cuts, music, color).
  • Uploads the finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel, with a title, description and thumbnail.
  • Can schedule the publication date of an uploaded video.

Who can use it

VideoEdit is not a public service. It runs locally on the owner's computer and is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel it is connected to.

Google account access

VideoEdit asks for permission to upload and manage videos on the connected YouTube channel. It uses this access only to perform the actions listed above, when the owner asks for them.

See the privacy policy for details.
